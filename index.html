What we do

One crew for sound, screens and stage.

One invoice, one number to call on the day, and the whole event filmed. Tell us the venue and how many guests, and we'll recommend the right setup in the quote.

Sound

Clear from the back row.

A PA sized to the room, so table twelve hears the speaker as clearly as the head table.

  • Wireless and wired mics
  • Podium mic and playback for films and nasheeds
  • Someone on the desk all event

Screens

Bright in a lit hall.

LED walls bright enough to read with the hall lights on, for your slides, your appeal film or a live feed of the stage.

  • LED walls 2m high, 3m or 6m wide
  • 65" TV for smaller rooms
  • Projector on a fast-fold screen

Stage

The room, transformed.

The front of the room set and lit, so the same hall looks like a different event.

  • Stage wash and uplighting
  • Backdrop and podium
  • A spotlight on the head table or charity stand

Questions

What organisers ask us.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the room, the date and what you need. Send us your date and venue and we'll come back with a full price the same day, with everything that's included written down.

What's included?

Every package includes delivery, setup, someone operating it throughout, pack-down and two microphones.

How far ahead should we book?

For Ramadan weekends and summer Saturdays, as early as you can; those dates go first. For other dates, a few weeks is usually fine. Check with us either way.

Do you need to visit the venue first?

Not to give you a price. If the hall is new to us, we'll ask a few questions or come and look before the day.

Can you play our appeal video or show a live feed?

Yes. Send us the file beforehand and we'll test it at soundcheck.

Our event is less than a week away.

Message us on WhatsApp rather than filling in the form, and we'll tell you straight away whether we can do it.

How do we pay?

A deposit to hold the date, and the balance on or before the event day.

Do we get the footage?

Yes, every event. Back within 48 hours, ready to post.
